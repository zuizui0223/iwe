# Replication discovery batch 002

Date: 2026-09-24  
Status: active discovery queue after timing-gate audit

## Why this batch exists

PR #11 removed the apparent one-step mixed and antagonist candidates that failed the frozen contemporaneous partner-window rule. The replication bottleneck is therefore **eligible-study discovery**, not effect conversion or variance imputation.

This batch broadens the search without broadening the estimand.

## Non-negotiable four-gate screen

A source can advance mixed #2 or antagonist #2 only if the same focal biological context supplies:

1. an independent programme/dependence cluster;
2. contemporaneously measured partner activity/availability sufficient to order plant timing as higher versus lower overlap;
3. final realized plant reproduction after the focal interaction cost; and
4. raw data or group summaries that support the frozen standardized-mean-difference family without invented variance.

Calendar date, attack rate, visitor presence, historical natural-history windows, or day/night exclusion do **not** substitute for gate 2.

## Search strata

### Mixed P1 — Yucca × yucca moth

Search `Tegeticula` and `Parategeticula` studies for designs that jointly measure adult moth activity, flower receptivity/flowering timing, and final viable seed output after larval consumption.

Reject:
- pollination/oviposition manipulations with no phenological contrast;
- moth abundance without matched plant reproduction;
- seed-set contrasts where treatment changes moth presence rather than timing.

### Mixed P1 — Ficus × agaonid fig wasp

Search for within-season or among-crop variation where receptive-fig timing is explicitly matched to pollinator availability and final seed production is quantified after wasp development.

Reject:
- wasp emergence/arrival phenology alone;
- crop abortion without a defensible final plant reproductive estimand;
- community entrainment studies where the focal outcome is wasp competition rather than plant reproduction.

### Mixed P2 — Trollius × Chiastocheta

IWE020 already shows that fly-presence contrasts can be SMD-ready while failing the timing exposure. Search only for a distinct publication/dataset that actually measures fly activity and flowering overlap.

### Mixed P2 — Lithophragma × Greya

IWE021 supplies cost/benefit and redundancy context but not strict synchrony. Search for a separate timing dataset; do not promote year or partner-presence contrasts.

### Antagonist P1 — Baptisia australis seasonal consumers

Evans, Smith & Gendron (1989), *Oecologia* 78:220–230, DOI `10.1007/BF00377159`, is a full-text target. The abstract-level evidence is promising because it reports three years of seasonal insect damage, late arrival of blister beetles, and effects on seed production.

Promotion requires identifying one focal antagonist whose measured activity window orders the plant timing contrast. A generic seasonal herbivore assemblage is not sufficient.

## Stop rules

- Do not convert a continuous slope into SMD merely to fill the replication target.
- Do not infer partner activity from damage, predation or the reproductive response.
- Do not use a different year’s partner activity to orient the focal year.
- Do not split sites/years within an existing programme to manufacture independence.
- Do not add a candidate as `ready` until all four gates are source-backed.
