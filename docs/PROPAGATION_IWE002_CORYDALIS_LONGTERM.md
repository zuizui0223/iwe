# IWE002 Kudo & Cooper 2019 — long-term Corydalis mismatch signal reaches final seed set

Date: 2026-10-07  
Study: Kudo & Cooper 2019, *Corydalis ambigua × Bombus*  
DOI: `10.1098/rspb.2019.0573`  
Propagation role: independent partner timing linked to final natural seed set over 19 years and in a flowering-time manipulation.

## Long-term design

At Nopporo, the programme monitored from 1999–2017:

- snowmelt timing;
- *C. ambigua* flowering onset;
- overwintered bumblebee emergence;
- natural seed set.

Flowering and bee emergence are measured independently of the reproductive outcome.

The source reports that early snowmelt increases the risk that flowering begins before bee emergence.

Natural seed production declines as this mismatch increases.

When flowering and bee emergence are close, seed set is high; when flowering precedes bees by roughly a week, natural seed set is much lower.

Thus the partner-relative timing signal is carried to final natural seed production.

## Snow-removal experiment

A snow-removal manipulation advances flowering and changes the plant–bee timing relation.

Across the pollination experiment, natural seed production is limited by pollination and falls when flowering occurs before bee emergence.

The experimental result therefore points in the same biological direction as the long-term monitoring:

`greater plant lead before bee emergence -> lower natural seed set`.

## Propagation state

The link is classified as **preserved**.

The upstream timing reference is `independent_partner_activity` because bee emergence is observed independently of seed production.

The final endpoint is natural seed set.

## Dependence

IWE002 extends the Nopporo/NFP series already represented by IWE001.

Both must remain in:

`DEP_CORYDALIS_KUDO_LONGTERM`.

IWE002 therefore adds a long-term propagation link but **does not add an independent programme** to programme-level retention counts.

## Claim boundary

The source mismatch axis is signed and spans both sides of matching in some components. This propagation adjudication does not convert the 19-year series into a new strict-H1 effect size or assume symmetry around zero mismatch.

It records only the source-backed biological direction relevant to the plant-leading domain:

> when flowering occurs before bumblebee emergence, increasing mismatch is associated with lower natural seed set.
