# Yucca filifera provenance closure — public full text exhausted

Date: 2026-09-26  
Candidate: `MIX002_YUCCA_FILIFERA`  
System: *Yucca filifera × Tegeticula yuccasella*  
Decision: retain P1 `blocked_timing_linkage`; the remaining blocker is an original fruit-provenance key

## Why this remains a strong P1 candidate

Unlike many mixed-system near-misses, this programme passes the independent partner-window gate.

During the 1998 flowering season, adult *T. yuccasella* were sampled with a UV light trap twice weekly from 5 March to 18 May. Adult density was highest in March; the article reports no moths in May even though *Y. filifera* flowers were still present.

The programme also measured post-cost plant reproduction. Mature fruits were dissected and seeds were classified as underdeveloped, mature viable, or mature damaged. Across 500 mature fruits, the public source reports pooled means and uncertainty for these seed categories.

So the missing object is not a new timing measurement and not a new reproductive endpoint. It is the linkage between them.

## What the long-form thesis adds

The 2000 UANL thesis makes the sampling architecture unusually clear:

- 20 plants were marked for reproductive phenology;
- panicles were collected every 10 days from March through August 1998 at Baño de San Ignacio and Campus Linares;
- 146 panicles were collected in total;
- 134 of those were scored for flower/fruit developmental state;
- 500 mature fruits were used for the seed-condition analysis;
- separate adult-moth trapping was conducted through the flowering season.

This confirms that date-resolved panicle sampling and final seed measurements coexisted in the same programme.

## Why the public thesis still cannot yield the strict SMD

The final seed table is pooled across 500 mature fruits. It does not report those fruits by:

- source plant;
- panicle;
- flowering cohort;
- panicle collection date; or
- month of the original flower.

The annex does not rescue the linkage. Tables 9.1–9.7 contain plant characteristics and counts of inflorescences/infructescences for the marked plants, but no fruit-level viable/damaged seed table keyed back to those plants or dates.

This matters because the measured partner window changes strongly through the season. March is high moth availability, whereas May retains flowers but has no trapped moths. Assigning pooled mature fruits to either window without provenance would manufacture the exposure grouping after the fact.

## Why fruit phenology is not enough

The thesis does retain plant- and date-resolved inflorescence/infructescence information. That cannot substitute for the missing final-seed linkage.

In this nursery-pollination system, the plant benefit and cost coexist inside fruits: pollination generates seeds and moth larvae consume a subset of those seeds. A mature-fruit count alone therefore does not measure the post-seed-predation reproductive balance represented by the pooled viable/damaged-seed table.

IWE must not promote fruit presence/counts to the final post-cost endpoint merely because those data have better provenance.

## Exact unlock datum

The candidate becomes extractable if one of the following is recovered:

1. the original fruit-level sheet containing plant/panicle or collection/flowering date plus viable and damaged seed counts; or
2. a source-backed final-seed summary stratified by a timing group that can be prospectively ordered from the adult-moth trapping series.

The most useful minimal columns would be:

`fruit_id, plant_id_or_panicle_id, collection_or_flowering_date, viable_seeds, damaged_seeds`

A date-only group summary could also suffice if its biological unit and variance are explicit.

## Operational consequence

The public article and public thesis are now exhausted as extraction sources. Re-searching or re-reading the same documents is not a completion route.

The next action is specifically an author/institutional archive request for the 1998 fruit-level laboratory/field records or an unpublished month/cohort summary.

Until that provenance is recovered, `MIX002_YUCCA_FILIFERA` remains P1 `blocked_timing_linkage` and contributes zero strict-H1 effects.
