# Suchan 2015 Trollius population phase-composition audit

Date: 2026-10-03  
Study: Suchan et al. 2015, *Trollius europaeus × Chiastocheta*  
DOI: `10.1002/ece3.1544`  
Decision: **do not use the eight-population dataset as a stage-timing-composition -> final-fitness test.**

## Why the route looked promising

The study follows eight *T. europaeus* populations across 15 population-year combinations and reports:

- Chiastocheta presence/absence;
- seed set before larval predation;
- a quantity called net seed set;
- seed germination in a four-population subset.

Because earlier work shows strong species differences in oviposition timing within the flower-age sequence, this dataset could in principle test whether populations dominated by early- versus late-ovipositing species differ in net plant fitness.

## Blocker 1 — timing guild composition is absent

The population-year table records Chiastocheta only as present or absent.

The flower-visitor surveys likewise pool the flies at the genus level.

No source-backed population-year composition of:

- early *C. rotundiventris*;
- intermediate species;
- late *C. dentifera* or other late species

is reported.

Species-specific timing information from other studies cannot be back-projected onto these populations.

## Blocker 2 — net seed set is model-derived

Seed set is measured before Chiastocheta larval predation.

The source then estimates net seed set from egg density using a previously published individual cost model:

`net seed set = seed set × (1 - 0.66 × x^0.26)`.

This is useful for the paper's presence/absence question but it is not a direct observed post-predation mature-seed endpoint.

## Consequence

The dataset can support:

- the ecological importance of Chiastocheta presence;
- evidence that alternative pollinators partly compensate when flies disappear;
- contextual variation in modeled pollination-benefit versus predation cost.

It cannot support the IWE confirmatory comparison:

> stage-timing composition -> observed final plant fitness.

No cross-study species assignment, no inferred timing-guild composition, and no relabelling of modeled net seed set as directly observed final reproduction are allowed.

This is a closed negative audit, not an unresolved data-access route.
