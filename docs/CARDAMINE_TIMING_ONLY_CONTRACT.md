# Cardamine timing-only exposure freeze\n\nDate: 2026-09-27  \nCandidate: ANT002_CARDAMINE_ANTHOCHARIS_2024  \nStatus: frozen before recovery of the missing adult-flight date table and before any individual response analysis\n\n## Purpose\n\nThis document freezes the first executable exposure rule for the Cardamine-Anthocharis raw-data route before the missing 2012-2014 female capture/recapture dates are recovered.\n\nThe rule uses timing only. It does not read egg load, larval stage, dehiscence success, intact reproductive units, or any other plant response/mechanism variable.\n\n## Source anchors\n\nDavies & Saccheri (2024) state that every focal ramet was followed from first flowering until dehiscence at 5-7 day intervals; newly flowering plants were individually labelled; the female butterfly flight season was determined from dates of capture and recapture of all females encountered in the Reserve; and the source flight-season boxplots use the median, 25th/75th percentiles, and 10th/90th percentile whiskers of the female capture+recapture date distribution.\n\n## Plant timing\n\nFor each year x ecotype x plant_id:\n\n> first_flowering_doy = minimum DOY with open flowers > 0.\n\nPre-flowering observations do not count as flowering. Egg presence cannot make a plant flowering and is not read by the timing helper.\n\n## Adult timing\n\nFor each study year independently, use all source-backed female capture + recapture event DOYs.\n\nDefine adult_q10_doy as the empirical 10th percentile and adult_q90_doy as the empirical 90th percentile.\n\nIf raw events are supplied, executable quantiles use linear interpolation (pandas default; equivalent to R type 7). The 10-90% boundaries are fixed because they reproduce the source's displayed flight-window whiskers rather than introducing a response-selected threshold.\n\nEarlier 2005-2010 MRR dates, male dates, pooled-sex first-capture summaries, and egg-laying dates are not admissible substitutes.\n\n## Frozen exposure classification\n\nWithin each year:\n\n- higher_synchrony: first flowering DOY lies inside or on the female q10-q90 flight window;\n- lower_synchrony: first flowering DOY lies before q10 or after q90.\n\nThis classification is two-sided by design: both early and late phenological escape are lower overlap with the adult flight window.\n\nThe executable helper retains ecotype. It does not pool early and late ecotypes and it does not estimate an effect.\n\n## What remains unfrozen\n\nThis timing freeze does not yet authorize an SMD.\n\nAfter the adult timing object is recovered, IWE must inspect the timing table without plant response columns and determine whether each year x ecotype stratum contains a defensible number of plants in both timing groups.\n\nOnly then may an outcome extraction rule be frozen. No stratum may be retained or dropped based on final reproductive values. No alternate percentile, cut-point, flowering-mode boundary, or response-driven binning is allowed after response inspection.\n\n## Executable implementation\n\nsrc/iwe/cardamine_timing.py provides first_flowering_dates(), adult_flight_windows(), and cardamine_timing_only_exposure().\n\nThe functions subset to timing columns before calculation. Regression tests verify that changing an extra intact_ru response column or adding egg counts cannot alter exposure assignments.\n\n## Fail-closed conditions\n\nThe timing stage stops if a plant year lacks a matching adult timing year; adult dates are not female 2012-2014 Dibbinsdale capture/recapture events (or an explicitly equivalent source-backed numeric window); the recovered object cannot reproduce a year-specific flight window without figure digitization; or the eventual timing table does not support a defensible categorical contrast within a source-compatible stratum.\n\nIn any of these cases Cardamine remains P1 blocked_timing_linkage; no SMD is manufactured.\n
