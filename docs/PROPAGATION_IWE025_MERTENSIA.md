# IWE025 Gallagher & Campbell 2021 — flowering timing reaches mature seed set, but visitation is not the dominant mediator

Date: 2026-10-07  
Study: Gallagher & Campbell 2021, *Mertensia ciliata*  
DOI: `10.3389/fevo.2021.641693`  
Propagation role: direct flowering-time manipulation carried to mature seed production under crossed water treatments.

## Design

Flowering onset and soil moisture were manipulated factorially.

Potted plants were induced to flower in four successive weekly cohorts. Each cohort was exposed to the field pollinator community for one week, after which flowers were bagged.

The source measured:

- probability of receiving a visit;
- visitation rate;
- visitor composition;
- mature seeds per marked flower;
- seed mass.

Plants remained under common post-pollination field conditions until mature seeds were collected.

Thus timing is prospectively defined by direct manipulation, and the endpoint is mature seed production.

## Temporal signal

Pollinator access declines through the experimental flowering season.

Between weeks 2 and 4, the probability that a plant receives a visit declines by about **61%**.

Among visited plants, per-flower visitation also declines after the first week.

Final seed production changes in the same broad temporal direction:

- flowering phenology has a significant effect on seed set;
- over four weeks, mean seed set declines by about **15%**;
- week 4 plants differ from the first two weeks in seed set.

This link is therefore classified as **preserved**:

`later experimental flowering -> reduced pollinator access -> lower mature seeds per flower`.

## Preservation does not imply a single mediator

The source explicitly warns against equating the final response with pollinator visitation alone.

The direct seed-set vs visitation-rate association is not significant:

- chi-square = **3.14**;
- **p = 0.08**.

Water availability has a larger effect on seed production and seed mass than flowering time:

- wet plants produce about **40% more seeds per flower** than dry plants;
- flowering-time and water effects on seed production are approximately additive.

Late plants also produce fewer but heavier seeds, so different reproductive components do not move identically.

Accordingly, the ledger records **preservation of temporal direction to mature seed number**, not proof that declining visitation is the sole causal pathway.

## Relation to IWE023

IWE025 and IWE023 are part of the same Gallagher–Campbell *Mertensia ciliata* research programme and conservatively share:

`DEP_MERTENSIA_GALLAGHER_CAMPBELL_RMBL`.

The programme now contains two different propagation outcomes:

- IWE023: a >5-fold visitation decline is **buffered** before seed set by changing pollinator effectiveness;
- IWE025: experimentally later flowering shows lower visitation and approximately 15% lower mature seed set, classified as **preserved**.

This within-programme heterogeneity is important. A prospective timing design does not have a fixed propagation state even in the same plant species.
