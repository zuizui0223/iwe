# Screening batch 004 — post-freeze mixed-system citation closure

Date: 2026-09-17
Scope: IWE041–IWE048

## Main result

This batch tested whether classic nursery-pollination systems supply the complete strict-H1 chain:

```text
plant-partner temporal matching
-> final net plant reproductive outcome
-> recoverable effect + uncertainty
```

They generally do not. The literature repeatedly contains one side of the chain without the other.

## IWE041 — Dianthus sylvestris × Hadena compta

Direct seasonal timing, Hadena seed predation and final female reproduction are measured in the same plants. Two annual slopes of relative reproductive success on flowering onset are quantitatively extractable:

- 2001: `beta = -0.037`, `SE = 0.009`;
- 2003: `beta = -0.076`, `SE = 0.030`.

These are stored in the timing-sensitivity lane because no independent moth-activity distribution is measured to convert calendar flowering onset into a strict plant–partner synchrony exposure.

## IWE042–043 — Silene × Hadena selection/host-choice studies

These studies directly resolve oviposition choice, seed predation, fruit initiation and/or selection through Hadena, but do not use plant–moth temporal matching as the focal exposure. They are mechanism/selection context rather than strict timing evidence.

## IWE044 — Silene alba / S. dioica × Hadena

Flowering timing, fruit set, fruit predation and undamaged-fruit success occur in the same field populations. The study therefore has unusually complete plant timing and final-fitness information, but no independently quantified moth-activity curve. Anther-smut infection is an additional interacting agent. It remains timing-sensitivity/context evidence.

## IWE045 — direct Hadena activity overlap, missing final fitness

This study directly compares host flowering periods with `Hadena bicruris` activity/oviposition. It supplies the timing measurement that IWE044 lacks, but does not supply a compatible final plant-fitness endpoint for strict H1.

Taken together, IWE044 and IWE045 illustrate the central mixed-system measurement split:

```text
paper A: plant timing + final fitness, weak partner-timing measurement
paper B: plant timing + partner activity, weak final-fitness measurement
```

Combining those papers as if they were one experiment is not permitted.

## IWE046–047 — linked mutualist/antagonist designs without timing exposure

Castilleja and Ipomopsis studies quantify pollinator and seed-predator pathways to female fitness, but the focal axes are floral traits/inflorescence size rather than temporal matching. They are retained as design context only.

## IWE048 — historical Corydalis precursor

An unusually early spring advanced flowering and reduced seed set in bee-pollinated spring ephemerals while fly-pollinated species were less affected. The study is useful historical context for the later direct Corydalis mismatch programme but lacks the explicit partner-timing exposure required by IWE strict H1.

## Prospective-integrity result

All IWE041–048 outcomes/design features were visible during post-freeze discovery before any hash-based holdout assignment. None can therefore be used as blind confirmation of the screening-informed nonlinear antagonist/mixed shape hypothesis, even where a hash would otherwise place the biological cluster in a reserved holdout stratum.
