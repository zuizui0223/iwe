Status: effect-size-ready mutualist boundary/null-leaning contrast; antagonist result retained qualitatively as a negative control.

## Pollinator-mediated flowering-date selection

In 2021, the study estimated selection on Julian date of floral anthesis under open pollination and hand outcross supplementation using the conditional component of zero-inflated Poisson models.

Source Table 2 reports:

- open-pollinated beta = +0.98, SE = 0.59;
- pollen-supplemented beta = +0.10, SE = 0.25;
- pollinator-mediated delta beta = OP - HP = **+0.88, SE = 0.64**;
- treatment x flowering-date P = **0.24**.

Positive native beta on Julian date favors later flowering. The IWE canonical coordinate is positive = an interaction-induced shift toward an earlier date, so the oriented effect is:

**-0.88, SE = 0.64.**

The effect is retained exactly because it is weak relative to its uncertainty; statistical significance is not an admission rule.

## Antagonist negative control

The same study tested whether naturally observed herbivore/florivore damage changed selection on floral traits. The source reports no evidence that antagonist damage altered either the strength or direction of selection on floral traits.

Flowering date was evaluated for antagonist-mediated selection in 2021, but the main article does not print a source-compatible signed damage contrast with SE in the main table. IWE therefore retains the antagonist result as a boundary statement rather than inventing a quantitative effect from absence of significance.

## Claim boundary

This study supports pollen limitation and a directly estimated pollinator-mediated flowering-date contrast, but the 2021 phenology contrast is uncertain and does not establish a strong shift.

It is valuable precisely as a design-matched boundary against positive-result filtering.

The pollen-supplementation contrast belongs to `DEP_TRILLIUM_KOSKI_2021`.
