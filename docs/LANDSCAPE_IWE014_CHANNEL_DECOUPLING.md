# IWE014 mixed-system channel decoupling

Date: 2026-10-02  
Study: Pettersson 1991, *Silene vulgaris × Hadena* spp.  
DOI: `10.1111/j.1600-0587.1991.tb00632.x`  
Landscape role: mixed benefit/cost timing evidence without an independent adult-activity curve.

## Source-level pattern

The study followed pollination and seed predation during two flowering seasons that differed strongly in pollinator abundance.

The published abstract reports four linked observations:

1. in the low-pollinator year, a lower percentage of flowers was pollinated;
2. nevertheless, similar numbers of flowers set fruit because fewer buds and flowers were consumed by *Hadena* larvae;
3. fewer seed capsules were preyed upon in that year, producing higher seed set;
4. across flowers opening at different parts of the season, pollination percentage and capsule destruction were positively correlated.

Most importantly for the landscape pivot, **early- and late-flowering individuals had similar pollination success, while early-flowering plants suffered higher seed predation**.

## Why this changes the mixed-system hypothesis

The original H2 treated mixed systems mainly as a possible quadratic net response to synchrony.

IWE014 points to a more informative structure:

> the positive and negative channels supplied by a pollinating seed predator need not have the same temporal shape.

For the early-versus-late comparison:

- benefit channel: approximately flat in pollination success;
- cost channel: higher early because seed predation is higher;
- net reproduction: therefore can shift even without a corresponding seasonal shift in pollination benefit.

This is **benefit-cost temporal decoupling**, not merely curvature in a single synchrony coefficient.

## Claim boundary

The study does not provide an independent focal-season adult-*Hadena* activity curve and therefore remains ineligible for strict H1.

The current accessible source summary also does not provide variance-bearing early/late final-seed summaries sufficient for a common-effect meta-analysis.

Accordingly IWE014 is registered as `channel_decoupling_evidence`, not as a quantitative strict effect.

Its value is mechanistic: the same partner lineage can generate benefit and cost channels whose seasonal responses are not interchangeable.
