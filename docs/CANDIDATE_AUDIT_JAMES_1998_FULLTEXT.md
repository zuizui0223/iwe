# Full-text audit — James 1998 Yucca cheater antagonist

Date: 2026-09-26  
Candidate: `ANT002_JAMES_1998_YUCCA`  
Source: James (1998) MSc thesis, Library and Archives Canada, MQ28950  
Decision: **retain P1 `blocked_summary_stats`; all three biological gates are source-confirmed**

## Why this candidate was re-opened

Earlier screening knew that the thesis linked non-pollinating yucca-moth timing to *Yucca kanabensis* reproduction, but the precise grain of the final seed observations was not yet established.

The public full thesis resolves that ambiguity.

## 1996 observational dataset

Chapter 4 describes one focal 1996 programme in which the investigators:

- recorded plant flowering start and end dates;
- followed the non-pollinating moth flight season daily;
- counted newly deposited oviposition droplets on developing fruit;
- harvested one or two fruit per plant after enough time for larval development;
- counted oviposition scars and larvae;
- counted **intact and damaged seeds** from those final fruit.

Thus plant timing, same-season antagonist activity and final post-damage reproduction coexist on the same plant-level programme.

The source also documents independent adult emergence timing in 1995 and 1996 rather than inferring the activity window from damaged seeds.

## Timing signal

The thesis contains source-defined timing effects:

- early- versus late-flowering plants differ in cheater oviposition in the experimental component;
- in the observational component, flowering date predicts the probability and number of ovipositions on fruit;
- the seasonal relation is biologically interpretable using the independently observed adult flight period.

This is substantially stronger than candidates where attack itself is used as the missing partner-activity curve.

## Why no SMD is extracted

The thesis records intact/damaged seed counts at plant level, but the published Chapter 4 results do **not** report the final seed outcome as:

- early versus late flowering group means + SD/SE + n;
- another source-defined high- versus low-overlap grouped final-seed contrast; or
- a test statistic that deterministically recovers the pooled within-group variance for such a contrast.

Instead, the timing-facing analyses are flowering-date/oviposition regressions and early/late oviposition comparisons. The final intact/damaged seed observations are not printed as the grouped table needed by the frozen SMD replication family.

IWE therefore will not:

- convert the continuous flowering-date regression to an SMD;
- use oviposition as the plant reproductive endpoint;
- infer early/late intact-seed means from figures;
- invent the missing within-group variance.

## Exact unlock condition

The candidate closes antagonist #2 if the original plant-level 1996 table or an author/source summary provides a defensible high/low synchrony grouping with final intact-seed (or equivalent net reproductive) means and variance.

Until then:

## Taxonomic note

The 1998 thesis predates later revision of the *Tegeticula yuccasella* complex and refers conservatively to an unnamed/non-pollinating yucca moth. Later Canadian syntheses connect this biology to the cheater lineage now called *T. corruptrix*. IWE retains the source-era identity rather than retroactively asserting an exact species assignment.
