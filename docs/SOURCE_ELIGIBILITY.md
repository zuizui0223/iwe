# IWE source-eligibility contract

Freeze date: 2026-09-18
Status: prospective; frozen before reading the reconstructed IWE078 timing-fitness effect

## Purpose

IWE's primary H1 is a meta-analysis of a reproducibly searchable published literature. A public dataset can make an effect technically reconstructable without making it part of that literature universe. Source eligibility is therefore separated from biological/effect eligibility.

This rule prevents a class with sparse published evidence from being filled post hoc by selectively adding unpublished datasets that were not searched symmetrically across all interaction classes.

## Primary H1 source types

A Tier-A effect may count toward the confirmatory primary H1 programme gate only when its biological study is documented in a formally published research source that can be recovered by the frozen literature-search workflow.

Eligible primary source forms are:

- peer-reviewed journal research articles;
- formally published proceedings/research papers with stable bibliographic identifiers;
- accepted/in-press research articles with a stable DOI and a public bibliographic record that identifies the empirical study.

The associated raw data may come from Zenodo, Dryad, GitHub, institutional repositories, supplementary files, or author archives. The location of the raw data does not change the publication eligibility of the biological study.

## Sources that do not enter the primary programme gate by themselves

The following remain outside the confirmatory primary H1 corpus unless a corresponding eligible research publication is verified:

- dataset-only repository records;
- analysis-code repositories without a published research article;
- conference abstracts or posters;
- theses/dissertations when no corresponding published article is identified;
- unpublished manuscripts or manuscript titles appearing only in repository metadata;
- personal webpages or informal reports.

These sources may be retained in a labelled provisional/grey-literature lane and used to:

- test whether an estimand can be reconstructed;
- audit measurement feasibility;
- run sensitivity analyses;
- identify candidate systems and future publications.

They must not increase the independent-programme count that opens the primary H1 gate.

## Preprints

Preprints are logged and may be quantitatively reconstructed, but they do not enter the confirmatory primary H1 programme count under this first contract. If IWE later decides to include preprints, that expansion must be applied prospectively and symmetrically to all three interaction classes with a reproducible preprint search, not introduced because one preprint supplies a needed effect.

## IWE078 consequence

The Zenodo record for Slimon & Agrawal (2026), *Phenological plasticity mediates sequential herbivory and fitness*, contains public empirical data and scripts but, at freeze time, no peer-reviewed or formally published research article under that exact study title has been verified.

Therefore:

- the frozen IWE078 reconstruction may be executed;
- its effect may be stored as provisional empirical evidence;
- its sign and magnitude may not determine whether the source is admitted;
- IWE078 does not count toward the primary antagonist programme gate unless a corresponding eligible publication is subsequently verified.

## Independence from results

This source rule is evaluated before effect sign, magnitude, significance, or compatibility with H1 is considered. No source is promoted or demoted because it strengthens or weakens the preferred biological pattern.
