# Replication discovery batch 002 — adjudication

Date: 2026-09-24  
Outcome: **no new strict SMD replication cluster recovered**

## Search result

The broadening step was useful because it tested whether the apparent scarcity was an artifact of focusing too narrowly on *Hadena*. It was not.

### Yucca × yucca moth

The literature contains strong measurements of active pollination, oviposition, flower/fruit retention and seed consumption. However, the targeted screen did not recover a study that simultaneously supplies:

- a focal-season adult moth activity curve;
- plant flowering/receptivity timing that can be prospectively ordered as higher versus lower overlap with that curve;
- final seed output after larval consumption; and
- an SMD-compatible variance route.

Pollination/oviposition timing alone is not the IWE exposure, and moth-presence manipulations cannot substitute for synchrony.

Decision: **no promoted candidate**.

### Ficus × agaonid wasps

Crop synchrony, receptive-phase timing, pollinator availability and wasp emergence are extensively studied. The strict bottleneck is the final plant endpoint: many papers target wasp competition, pollinator limitation, crop abortion, or community persistence rather than a variance-bearing final seed outcome tied to a more-versus-less matched crop contrast.

Decision: **no promoted candidate**.

### Trollius × Chiastocheta

Re-screening did not identify a distinct dataset beyond the already known cost/benefit and fly-presence literature that closes the timing gate. IWE020 remains useful context but presence/absence is not phenological synchrony.

Decision: **no promoted candidate**.

### Lithophragma × Greya

The literature strongly addresses conditional mutualism, redundancy and seed consumption. No distinct programme was recovered that jointly measures contemporaneous *Greya* activity, plant timing overlap and final reproduction on the frozen SMD route.

Decision: **no promoted candidate**.

### Baptisia australis seasonal consumers

Evans, Smith & Gendron (1989) is ecologically relevant but does not isolate one focal antagonist whose measured activity window orders plant flowering timing. It describes seasonal turnover in multiple flower/seed consumers and the resulting damage/seed-production pattern.

Decision: **reject from strict antagonist synchrony target**.

## Interpretation of the negative screen

The scarcity is now itself well characterized:

> the literature often measures **plant seasonal timing + interaction outcome**, or **partner phenology + interaction occurrence**, or **final reproduction + partner presence**, but rarely all three links needed for `partner window -> synchrony -> final plant reproduction` in one variance-bearing design.

This does not justify relaxing the primary estimand. It changes the next task.

## Next search strategy

Do not continue broad taxonomic browsing. Search by **design signature** instead:

- phrases combining `activity period`, `flowering phenology`, `seed production`, `seed predator`;
- experiments explicitly labelled early/peak/late where animal abundance is measured in the same season;
- archived raw datasets in studies currently classed as direct seasonal timing;
- citations of papers that explicitly calculate plant–animal phenological overlap/synchrony.

Priority remains mixed #2, then antagonist #2. A candidate enters the ledger only after the timing gate is demonstrated, not before.
