# Silene ciliata mixed-candidate timing audit

Date: 2026-09-24  
Source: Giménez-Benavides et al. (2007), *Oikos* 116:1461–1472, DOI `10.1111/j.0030-1299.2007.15644.x`  
Candidate: `MIX002_SILENE_CILIATA`  
Decision: **reject from the strict phenological-synchrony replication target**

## What the experiment actually manipulates

The study is an informative nursery-pollination experiment in the independent Iberian *Silene ciliata × Hadena consparcatoides* system. The authors exposed plants to non-overlapping pollinator regimes during the day and night and followed reproductive performance from flowers through seeds/seedlings.

The doctoral-thesis methods give the decisive design detail:

- 104 adult plants were randomly selected in a fenced natural population;
- 35 were assigned to nocturnal pollination, 35 to diurnal pollination, and 33 to open pollination;
- fine-mesh tents were used to control visitor access;
- the treatment therefore changes **which part of the daily pollinator assemblage can visit**, not when the plant flowers within its season.

The article reports that diurnal access yields higher fruit set, nocturnal access yields higher brood size, overall fecundity is similar, and *Hadena* seed predation pushes the nursery interaction toward parasitism.

## Why this does not satisfy IWE strict H1

IWE's frozen primary estimand is the reproductive effect of **phenological synchrony/overlap**. For experimental timing contrasts, a treatment can enter strict H1 when plant timing is manipulated and contemporaneous partner availability lets treatments be ordered prospectively as more versus less matched.

The day-only/night-only exclusion design does not alter flowering onset, peak, seasonal position, or plant–partner lag. It manipulates access within the 24-hour cycle and consequently changes pollinator assemblage composition.

Promoting this contrast would silently broaden the project from phenological synchrony to arbitrary temporal niche/exclusion contrasts after screening. That is not allowed.

## Gate decision

| replication gate | decision |
|---|---|
| independent programme from IWE015 | yes |
| qualifying phenological timing window | **no** |
| post-interaction plant reproduction | yes |
| SMD-ready summary statistics | not relevant to strict target |
| strict replication status | **rejected_timing** |

The paper remains useful as mechanism/context evidence for mixed nursery pollination, especially the pollination-benefit versus seed-predation-cost decomposition. It simply does not reduce the two-independent-clusters/class gap.

## Search consequence

The mixed #2 search remains centered on `IWE014` (*Silene vulgaris × Hadena*) and other **seasonal** mixed-system programmes where flowering position/overlap is actually measured or manipulated and linked to final post-predation reproduction.
