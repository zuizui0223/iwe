# IWE004 Kehrberger & Holzschuh 2019 — early seasonal service signal reaches seed set

Date: 2026-10-07  
Study: *Pulsatilla vulgaris*  
DOI: `10.1038/s41598-019-51916-0`  
Propagation role: seasonal-position signal carried through realized bee visitation to seed set.

## Source pattern

Across eight grasslands, bee abundance and pollinator-suitable hours increase through the season, but visitation to focal *P. vulgaris* flowers is highest early and declines later as the number of co-flowering species increases.

Estimated total bee visits per flower are positively related to seed set.

For open-pollinated flowers, seed set declines marginally with later bud opening (F=2.8, p=0.098), whereas hand-pollinated seed set does not change with bud-opening date (F=0.6, p=0.460).

When estimated total bee visits and Julian date are entered together, Julian date adds no explanatory power, indicating that the seasonal seed-set relation is mediated primarily through realized visitation rather than calendar date itself.

## Propagation state

The seasonal timing signal is classified as **preserved**:

`early seasonal position -> higher realized visitation -> higher seed set`.

The upstream reference is `seasonal_position_only`, not independent partner activity, because total bee abundance itself rises over the season and does not provide a monotonic partner-availability window.

## Claim boundary

The final date effect is marginal when analysed directly, and the study is observational across seasonal position rather than an experimental timing manipulation.

The propagation state therefore records biological direction, not a strict synchrony effect or causal estimate of calendar date.
