# Hurlburt 2004 — no-contact archival provenance route (2026-10-09)

**Status:** original Hurlburt dissertation recovered and directly audited;
the original 1999–2002 Onefour marked-flower × female census ×
**post-larval viable-seed** linkage remains **unrecovered**. This page
records a **finite source-informed archival search route**, not a new
biological result or an author-contact workflow.

## What the primary thesis actually establishes

Original University of Alberta thesis: DOI
[10.7939/r3-fe1d-kj80](https://doi.org/10.7939/r3-fe1d-kj80),
institutional [Scholaris item](https://ualberta.scholaris.ca/items/6a763405-6a9c-4205-aaf5-25b7eac09b3d).
Physical PDF pages 85–87 (printed 73–75) document individual marked
flower opening, the copied flower→ovary number, flower abortion/
retention, and an adult female *Tegeticula* census inside fresh flowers.
They **do not** publish a matching raw table of every marked flower's
calendar date and intact viable mature seeds.

The thesis's 2004 Methods and 2007 government fruit summaries therefore
cannot be spliced into an SMD by assuming source IDs, zero seeds on
unobserved aborted units, or calendar observations on unsampled dates.
Table 3.10's nonsignificant runs tests are neither equivalence tests
nor a within-plant date→intact seed result.

## Newly identified **distinct documents**, not data files

The official [2011 Canada recovery strategy](https://www.canada.ca/en/environment-climate-change/services/species-risk-public-registry/recovery-strategies/soapweed-yucca-glauca-moth-tegeticula-yuccasella-2011.html)
explicitly names:

1. Hurlburt 2005, *Monitoring protocol for Soapweed (Yucca glauca)
   and Yucca Moths (Tegeticula yuccasella) in Alberta*, **draft,
   21 pp**, submitted to the Alberta Soapweed and Yucca Moth Recovery Team.
   Potential **codebook/protocol custodian** for identity/grain questions,
   **not** verified original 1999–2002 field data.
2. Hurlburt 2007, *Yucca moth (Tegeticula yuccasella) population
   assessment and monitoring methodology at AAFC Onefour Research
   Substation, Alberta in 2007*, **final report, 42 pp**, prepared for
   Agriculture and Agri-Food Canada. Potential AAFC **collection/stewardship**
   lead, but 2007 is **not** one of the original Onefour 1999–2002
   marked-flower years.
3. Hurlburt 2011, *Field Verification Report on Soapweed and Yucca Moths
   in Canada*, **7 pp**, cited in the official
   [2013 Soapweed status bibliography](https://www.canada.ca/en/environment-climate-change/services/species-risk-public-registry/cosewic-assessments-status-reports/soapweed-2013/chapter-14.html).
   A later monitoring-source lead only.

The 2006 Alberta recovery plan embedded in the federal recovery
strategy also calls for *monitoring currently marked individual
Soapweed plants across seasons and years*. This shows institutional
monitoring continuity was an explicit objective; it **does not verify
that original flower-number IDs or marked-fruit seed records were
archived or preserved**.

  No access-to-information application is submitted by IWE.
- Alberta recovery-team reports, especially the *2005 draft monitoring
  protocol* and *2007 AAFC final report*, are exact archival document
  targets; both are **citation-verified but full-text unrecovered** in
  the current public-source search.

No email, author communication, official-record submission, or access
request has been sent. Do not construe a government citation as proof
that a government institution possesses all original thesis raw data.

## Finite retrieval decision and scientific stop rule

1. Find the **actual original 1999–2002 Onefour** date-stamped female
   moth census sheet with denominators/effort; do not substitute a
   2005/2007 monitoring sheet or a site-year moth average.
2. Find marked flowering-unit identity, flowering date, flower-fate
   ledger and the same unit's **post-larval viable-seed count** (or
   timing-cohort final seed means/SD tied to adult census and appropriate
   unit). If the only available marked outcomes are fruit
   retention/abortion or counts of larvae/oviposition punctures,
   document a **fruit-fate pathway** but do not advertise a final
   intact-seed fitness effect.
3. Require original ID lineage `year × clone × inflorescence ×
   marked flower` (or defensible equivalent), record unmatched /
   missing aborted units, and preserve clone-level dependence.
4. If these documents expose only templates, cohort-free mature-seed
   averages or later-year monitoring, mark that archive route
   **exhausted_no_same_unit_seed_data**, keep
   `MIX002_HURLBURT_2004` blocked, and pursue another independent
   mixed-system programme **without relaxing strict H1**.
