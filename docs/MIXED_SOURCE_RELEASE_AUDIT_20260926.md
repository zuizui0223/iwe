# Mixed source-release audit — Hurlburt and eastern Joshua tree

Date: 2026-09-26  
Status: no new strict mixed SMD cluster

## Hurlburt 2007 does not close the Hurlburt 2004 archival blocker

The Canadian status synthesis confirms that the Onefour programme has the right overall architecture:

- in 1999–2003 adult *Tegeticula yuccasella* were counted in roughly 100 fresh flowers several times per week during flowering;
- marked clones/inflorescences were followed to fruit set;
- 30–200 mature fruits per year were dissected;
- annual fruit, oviposition, larval and moth-density indices were retained.

However, the 2007 follow-up is not a replacement for the unrecovered 2004 thesis. The public synthesis states that the 2007 adult moth assessment was carried out only during a **week-long site visit**. Its annual indices (for example fruit/clone and moths/flower) therefore cannot provide the season-wide high-versus-low plant–moth overlap contrast required by the frozen strict-window contract.

Decision: keep `MIX002_HURLBURT_2004` as P1 `blocked_timing_linkage`. Do not turn annual moth abundance into synchrony.

## Eastern Joshua tree USGS programme — ideal design, unreleased source

The USGS Southwest Biological Science Center began a field study of *Yucca jaegeriana × Tegeticula antithetica* in 2022 explicitly around the maintenance of phenological synchrony.

The project design is unusually close to IWE's four-gate target:

- moth visitation is measured with traps attached to flowering inflorescences;
- reproductive ecology and pod production are followed on the same sample trees;
- harvested pods are evaluated for fertile seeds;
- larval damage is recorded;
- 2022 and 2023 provide contrasting flowering and moth-visit conditions.

The 2023 ESA poster reports site-level means/SD from the pilot, but it explicitly labels the results **preliminary and do not cite**. A later 2025 conference abstract reports 56 detected moths in 2022 versus 14 in 2023 and lower viable-pod production in the second year, but it still does not expose a citable unit-level synchrony-to-final-seed effect and sampling variance.

No final primary publication or pollination-data release was recovered in the 2026-09-26 search. The active USGS project page still describes the work as ongoing.

Decision: add `MIX002_JOSHUA_USGS_2022_2023` as P2 `blocked_source_release`.

## Why this distinction matters

An unreleased near-perfect design is not equivalent to a published but statistically incomplete study.

IWE now distinguishes:

- `blocked_timing_linkage` — the source exists, but the timing and final-fitness quantities cannot yet be linked at the correct unit;
- `blocked_source_release` — the research design appears capable of satisfying the contract, but the final citable quantitative source is not yet available.

Neither status advances the independent-cluster count.
