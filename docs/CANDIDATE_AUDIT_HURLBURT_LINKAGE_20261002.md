# Hurlburt 2004 secondary-source linkage audit

Date: 2026-10-02  
Candidate: `MIX002_HURLBURT_2004`  
System: *Yucca glauca × Tegeticula yuccasella*  
Decision: retain P1 `blocked_timing_linkage`; narrow the unlock to the mature-fruit identifier join.

## What is now source-backed without the thesis PDF

Authoritative Canadian status material derived from the Hurlburt programme establishes all three measurement layers inside the same Onefour programme.

### Adult partner timing

During Soapweed flowering in 1999–2003, all adult Yucca Moths present in approximately 100 fresh flowers across the Onefour site were counted several times per week.

This is an independent focal-season adult-activity series. It is not reconstructed from host egg receipt or seed damage.

### Marked plant reproduction

A subset of individually marked Soapweed clones and inflorescences was followed through fruit set.

Thus the programme used biological identifiers capable, in principle, of carrying plant flowering units forward into reproduction.

### Mature fruit / seed outcomes

Each year approximately 30–200 mature Soapweed fruits were dissected. Public summaries preserve oviposition and larval outcomes from those mature fruits, and Hurlburt-derived population summaries report viable seeds per fruit with variance.

For Onefour 1999–2003, the public synthesis reports:

- viable seeds per fruit = 146.3 ± 93.9 SD, n = 221 fruits;
- fruits per flowering clone = 3.76 ± 0.63.

These quantities prove that final mature-fruit reproduction was measured with variance.

## The remaining uncertainty is now one join key

The public derivative sources describe marked clones/inflorescences followed for fruit set and mature fruits dissected annually, but they do not state that the dissected mature-fruit records retain:

- clone / inflorescence identifier;
- flower or cohort date;
- or another source-defined key that maps the mature fruit back to its position on the flowering × adult-moth activity curve.

Therefore the candidate is no longer blocked by missing adult timing or missing final reproduction. It is blocked by **provenance of the join between those two layers**.

## Exact thesis audit test

The public thesis can promote this route only if a table, appendix, methods statement, or unit-level data layout establishes at least one of:

1. mature-fruit records retain marked clone/inflorescence IDs and those marked units have source-backed flowering dates;
2. mature viable/damaged seed summaries are reported by flowering date or flowering cohort that can be ordered against the contemporaneous adult-moth series;
3. a source-defined timing/overlap group is followed directly to mature fruit or viable-seed output with variance.

If the thesis only provides:

- repeated adult moth dates;
- marked-unit fruit set;
- and a separate pooled sample of mature fruit;

then the route remains blocked, because those layers cannot be joined post hoc.

## What must not be done

- Do not use annual moths-per-flower as phenological synchrony.
- Do not correlate annual adult abundance with annual viable seeds and call it overlap.
- Do not assign the pooled 221 Onefour fruits to early/late flowering without a source-backed key.
- Do not infer fruit identity from the fact that both marked clones and fruit dissections occurred in the same programme.
